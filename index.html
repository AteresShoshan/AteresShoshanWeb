Our Mission
A true מקום תורה — where every
בת ישראל can thrive
Every בת ישראל deserves the opportunity to flourish—academically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually.

You know her potential. So do we. You've watched her struggle with homework that takes three times longer than it should. You've sat across from teachers who mean well but don't have the tools to reach her. You've worried about her confidence, her friendships, her future.

Learning differences are not a reflection of intelligence, motivation, or potential. They are simply differences in how students process and engage with information. At Ateres Shoshana, our approach is designed around the student, not the system.

Ateres Shoshana was created for the girl who needs more than a mainstream classroom can offer. A bright, capable student who deserves a full, joyful high school experience rooted in Torah values, with the support designed around how she learns.

Our Approach
A Strengths-Based Model
Every student's unique learning profile is recognized and supported. Each accommodation is thoughtfully woven into the school day so she can access the full curriculum in the way that works best for her—by design.

Individualized Support

Confidence Building

Building skills, self-awareness, and resilience for success in college, careers, and adult life—as wives, mothers, and integral members of the community.

Highly trained special education teachers with proven track records, plus newer educators bringing the latest methods. This school was designed from the ground up for how your daughter learns.
