Welcome to Ateres Shoshana

Many students come to Ateres Shoshana after years of struggling in school. Ateres Shoshana can, however, confidently tell parents that it is uniquely equipped to help their daughters reach their full potential. For all students, it means gaining a positive self-image, an understanding that they can shine, and a belief that they are valued members of our Jewish community.

Key to our success is the embrace and support from the communities. The cost of providing special education, and especially Jewish special education, is prohibitive, and without broad community support would be out of reach for many, if not most, of the families we serve.

We invite you to browse these pages to learn more about the multitude of ways in which you can make a real difference in the lives of our students and their families.

Questions About Giving

Find answers about giving to Ateres Shoshana and how your support helps.

Are donations tax-deductible?
Yes. Ateres Shoshana is part of P'TACH, which is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt for your records.
How does my gift help?
Your contribution directly funds scholarships, specialized instruction, and the resources that help students with dyslexia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia, auditory and language processing challenges, Nonverbal Learning Disabilities (NVLD), executive functioning difficulties, ADHD and memory-related challenges succeed. Every dollar strengthens our mission.
Can I give monthly?
Absolutely. Monthly giving provides steady support throughout the year and sustains our work with students. Set up a recurring donation at whatever level feels right for you.
Can I sponsor a specific program?
Yes. We welcome restricted gifts to support particular initiatives that matter to you. Contact our administration to discuss options that align with your interests.

Why Your Gift Matters

Every student at Ateres Shoshana deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and succeed in an environment designed for their unique needs. Your support helps provide specialized instruction, individualized attention, and a school where students can build the skills and confidence needed for lifelong success.
